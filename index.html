The thinking behind the work
A website should do more than exist.

It should create a reason to believe.

Maidaula Websites is the independent web design and development practice of Ibrahim Hanif Maidaula. The focus is straightforward: make the online experience clearer, more useful and more memorable.

01

Designed around people

Good design is not decoration. Navigation, hierarchy and calls-to-action are considered from the visitor's point of view.

02

Built for every screen

The experience is designed to adapt across phones, tablets, laptops, desktops and Chromebook-sized displays.

03

Performance matters

Heavy pages lose attention. I prioritize sensible assets, efficient code and a clean structure wherever possible.

04

Professional communication

You get a clear process and a straightforward way to discuss your project before anything is built.

05

Honest portfolio

The projects shown here are clearly presented as demonstrations. I do not claim client results, testimonials or partnerships that I do not have.

Workflow / 6 stages

From first thought
to live website.

A clear path from the first conversation to the finished website. Each stage has a purpose, so the project stays understandable.

01

Discover

Understand your goals, audience, content, style and what the website needs to accomplish.

02

Plan

Turn the requirements into a clear page structure, user journey and visual direction.

03

Design

Create a polished interface with strong typography, spacing, hierarchy and responsive behavior.

04

Develop

Build the approved experience and make the interactions work across modern screen sizes.

05

Review

Test the website, review the details and make agreed adjustments before launch.

06

Launch

Prepare the final website for its intended hosting environment and hand over the finished work.
